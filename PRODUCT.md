# Product

Layer Consulting: the personal advisory practice of Franco Puppo, presented at layerconsulting.net.

<!-- Facts marked (inferred) were taken from the conversation and the LayerItaly repos, not from an interview. Confirm or correct them. -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, no build step. Served by GitHub Pages (planned). Two languages: Italian at `/`, English at `/en/`.

## Users

Founders, CEOs, boards and leadership teams of Italian and international companies that face a technology or AI decision they cannot take alone (inferred). They read on a laptop between meetings, or on a phone after a referral.

## Product Purpose

Make a senior decision-maker want a first conversation with Franco. Success is an email to franco.puppo@layerconsulting.net.

## Positioning

A personal brand, not an agency: one senior advisor who decides with the client and then, through Layer, builds what was decided. Higher level than a dev shop: decisions, risk and outcomes, not technology lists. The user asked for "più consulenziale, più personal branded, più alto livello".

## Operating Context

Three engagement formats: Advisory (strategy and roadmap), Fractional CTO, Delivery with Layer. Domains with real work behind them: football data and AI scouting, legal and financial AI agents, consumer digital products.

## Capabilities and Constraints

No contact form or backend: contact is by email. No analytics, no cookies. Client names are not public.

## Brand Commitments

- Layer mark: five stacked rounded bars, the top one shorter with a cyan to magenta gradient (#38e0dc, #7a7cf0, #c04ae8).
- Typography: the user approved Inter Tight / Inter (Apple-like sans) and rejected the serif.
- Photography: black-and-white portraits in a Jony Ive register (turtleneck on white, design studio), from ~/Desktop/ritratti-ai. The user rejected the suit-and-tie portraits.
- Motion: the user explicitly asked for a modern layout "with everything moving".

## Evidence on Hand

Ventures live and public: Trailgun (trailgun.ai), Weaver (weaverplay.com). No client logos, no testimonials, no metrics cleared for publication.

## Product Principles

- The problem first, then the technology.
- Real numbers, no magic: AI reasons, code does the sums.
- Whoever advises, answers for it, through to production.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, every animation collapses under prefers-reduced-motion, readable without JS.
