Every decision has five layers.

Most people only look at the one on top. I start from the bottom, because that is where it is decided whether the AI will hold.

  1. Artificial intelligence

    Judgement that scales. It comes last, and it holds only if the rest is solid.

  2. Product

    What people actually use, every day.

  3. Data

    Sources you can check, linked together. Code does the sums, not the model.

  4. Architecture

    A system that carries growth without letting costs run away.

  5. Strategy

    What has to change in the business, and why now.

When it makes sense to call me.

AI is on the agenda, strategy isn't.

The board wants a plan for artificial intelligence. You need to know where it adds margin, where it is only a demo, and which use case to start from.

A project is off course.

Slipping deadlines, growing costs, suppliers that never converge. You need an independent diagnosis and a plan to bring it back on track.

There is an investment to assess.

Before acquiring a company or funding a digital product, you need to know what is underneath: architecture, technical debt, team, scalability.

You need senior leadership, not a full-time CTO.

The business is growing faster than its technical organisation. You need someone to lead the choices, the team and the suppliers, at the right commitment.

Artificial intelligence doesn't replace judgement. It makes it scale.
