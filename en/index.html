When to call me

The moments when outside judgement makes the difference.

It is rarely more technology that is missing. What is missing is someone who can tell what matters from what is noise, and who takes responsibility for the call.

i.

AI is on the agenda, strategy isn't

The board wants a plan for artificial intelligence. You need to know where it adds margin, where it is only a demo, and which use case to start from.

ii.

A project is off course

Slipping deadlines, growing costs, suppliers that never converge. You need an independent diagnosis and a plan to bring it back on track.

iii.

An investment to assess

Before acquiring a company or funding a digital product, you need to know what is really underneath: architecture, technical debt, team, scalability.

iv.

Senior leadership without a full-time CTO

The business is growing faster than its technical organisation. You need someone to lead the choices, the team and the suppliers, at the right commitment.

Artificial intelligence doesn't replace judgement. It makes it scale.

Approach

Three rules I don't negotiate.

They hold for a half-day opinion as much as for a year-long programme.

01

The problem first, then the technology

Every recommendation starts from what has to change in your business on Monday morning, not from which tool is in fashion.

02

Real numbers, no magic

AI reasons and explains; the data and the sums come from code, on sources you can check. What cannot be measured, I don't call a result.

03

Whoever advises, answers for it

I don't hand over slides and disappear. Whoever designs the strategy sees it through to production, and answers for it.
